What happens in the room

This is not a talk. You work on your own project, with our developers next to you, and we make progress on the call.

  1. You show us where it is

    Your idea, your half-built app, or the site you have had for years. Whatever state it is in.

  2. We work on it together

    Small breakout groups by need: getting live, infrastructure, reliability, user experience, go to market.

  3. You leave unblocked

    Something deployed, a decision made, or a clear next step you can do yourself. Plus a team you can come back to.

Who it is for

  • You have an idea and AI already told you how to build it, but it is still not live, robust, or scalable.
  • You run a small business or nonprofit whose site was built years ago and the bar has moved.
  • You started a project, a launch or a campaign and stalled at the last technical steps.
