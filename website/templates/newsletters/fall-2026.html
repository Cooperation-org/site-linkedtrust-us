A word from the CEO

As the CEO for LinkedTrust.us I am immensely proud of our team, our ability to adapt and create value in today’s rapidly changing environment while staying true to our core mission.

Our team is helping startups and small businesses to leverage agentic AI and turn their expertise into products. Our vision for validating impact through decentralized trust is a reality with trusted testimonials. And we have taken the time and effort for us transfer our culture to something meaningful: Workers.VC is automating our methods of fast, fair, equitable worker ownership.

To help every startup we have opened up a monthly live hands-on workshop: LEVELUP. Our workshop was a great success for the attendees. Join our next workshop on October 21, share the code LT-INSIDER to register for free.

Truly enjoyed sharing time on the UNMUTED podcast in August, and looking forward to presenting our work at Ownership Economy Oct 1 in Manhattan and sharing our approach to validating impact at SOCAP26 in Chicago this fall.

Gitonga · CEO, LinkedTrust
